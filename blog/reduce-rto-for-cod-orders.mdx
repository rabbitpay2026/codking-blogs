      <div className="ck-body">
        <p className="ck-lede">Return to Origin can happen for many reasons, including incorrect addresses, weak purchase intent, communication gaps, and failed delivery attempts. Reducing avoidable RTO requires attention across the full COD journey.</p>
        <h2 id="confirm-intent">Confirm customer intent early</h2>
        <p>A clear confirmation step before fulfillment can help teams identify orders that need attention before packing and shipping costs are committed.</p>
        <h2 id="address-quality">Improve address quality</h2>
        <p>Capture complete addresses and a reachable phone number. Clear information reduces avoidable delivery confusion.</p>
        <h2 id="communication">Keep delivery communication clear</h2>
        <p>Set expectations about dispatch and delivery and make legitimate order communication easy to recognize.</p>
        <h2 id="review">Review patterns, not isolated failures</h2>
        <p>Look for repeated issues by region, product, order value, or workflow stage and improve the process where patterns appear.</p>
