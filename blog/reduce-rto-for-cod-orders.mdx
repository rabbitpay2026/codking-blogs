<div className="max-w-3xl mx-auto"><header className="mb-10"><div className="text-sm font-bold text-[#734DFF]">COD & RTO</div><h1 className="text-4xl md:text-5xl font-bold tracking-tight mt-4">How to Reduce RTO for COD Orders</h1><p className="text-xl text-gray-600 dark:text-gray-300 mt-5">Practical operational steps that can improve COD order quality before and after fulfillment begins.</p><div className="text-sm text-gray-500 mt-6">COD King Team · July 2026 · 7 min read</div></header><div className="aspect-video rounded-3xl bg-gradient-to-br from-[#734DFF] to-[#3613B6] flex items-center justify-center text-white mb-12"><div className="text-center"><div className="text-sm font-bold tracking-[0.2em] opacity-80">COD KING BLOG</div><div className="text-3xl font-bold mt-3">Reducing COD RTO</div></div></div><section><h2>Introduction</h2><p>Return to Origin occurs when a shipped order cannot be successfully delivered and returns to the seller. For COD orders, prevention is an important operational priority.</p><h2>Improve order quality before shipping</h2><p>Start by checking whether the order information is usable. A complete address, reachable phone number, appropriate postal code, and clear customer intent give fulfillment teams a stronger foundation.</p><h2>Communicate before the delivery attempt</h2><p>Customers should know that their order was accepted, when it is expected to move, and what they need to do at delivery.</p><h2>Use operational rules consistently</h2><p>Merchants can define internal rules for verification, manual review, address correction, or cancellation and review those rules as order patterns change.</p><h2>Learn from RTO reasons</h2><p>Track whether failures result from incorrect addresses, unreachable customers, refusal at delivery, or other logistics issues.</p><h2>Key takeaways</h2><ul><li>Validate useful order information before fulfillment.</li><li>Confirm intent when appropriate.</li><li>Keep customers informed before delivery.</li><li>Track RTO reasons.</li></ul><h2>Conclusion</h2><p>Reducing RTO is an ongoing operational process combining cleaner order data, appropriate confirmation, communication, and review.</p></section></div>
