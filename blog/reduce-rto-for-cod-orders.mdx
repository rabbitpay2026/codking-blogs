  <Lead>Return to Origin can happen for many reasons, including incorrect addresses, weak purchase intent, communication gaps, and failed delivery attempts. Reducing avoidable RTO requires attention across the full COD journey.</Lead>

  <H2 id="confirm-intent">Confirm customer intent early</H2>

  A clear confirmation step before fulfillment can help teams identify orders that need attention before packing and shipping costs are committed.

  <H2 id="address-quality">Improve address quality</H2>

  Capture complete addresses and a reachable phone number. Clear information reduces avoidable delivery confusion.

  <H2 id="communication">Keep delivery communication clear</H2>

  Set expectations about dispatch and delivery and make legitimate order communication easy to recognize.

  <H2 id="review">Review patterns, not isolated failures</H2>

  Look for repeated issues by region, product, order value, or workflow stage and improve the process where patterns appear.
