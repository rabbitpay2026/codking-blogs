        <p className="ck-lede">Cash on Delivery cancellations can happen before dispatch or during the fulfillment journey. A clearer confirmation process and better communication can help merchants identify genuine purchase intent earlier.</p>
        <h2 id="confirm-intent">Confirm customer intent</h2><p>Confirming the order before fulfillment helps identify customers who genuinely intend to receive their COD order.</p>
        <h2 id="order-details">Verify important order details</h2><p>Make sure the customer has provided a reachable phone number and clear delivery information before the order moves forward.</p>
        <h2 id="communication">Communicate clearly</h2><p>Keep customers informed about confirmation, dispatch, and delivery so they know what to expect from the order.</p>
        <h2 id="patterns">Review cancellation patterns</h2><p>Look for repeated cancellation patterns across products, locations, order values, and workflow stages to identify where improvements are needed.</p>
