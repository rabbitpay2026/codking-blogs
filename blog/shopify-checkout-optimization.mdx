  <Lead>Checkout optimization is about helping a shopper complete an intended purchase with as little confusion as possible. Clear information, predictable choices, and focused interfaces are more useful than unnecessary steps.</Lead>

  <H2 id="clarity">Prioritize clarity</H2>

  Make prices, delivery expectations, payment choices, and required information easy to understand.

  <H2 id="friction">Remove unnecessary friction</H2>

  Every additional field or decision should have a clear purpose.

  <H2 id="mobile">Design for mobile shoppers</H2>

  Use readable type, comfortable tap targets, and a clear visual hierarchy on smaller screens.

  <H2 id="measure">Measure the complete journey</H2>

  Review where shoppers hesitate or abandon and test changes carefully.
