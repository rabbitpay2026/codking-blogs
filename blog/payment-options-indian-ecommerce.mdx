        <p className="ck-lede">Indian e-commerce shoppers may prefer different payment methods depending on trust, convenience, order value, and familiarity. A good checkout presents those choices clearly without overwhelming the customer.</p>
        <h2 id="choices">Present choices clearly</h2>
        <p>Use familiar labels and make the difference between prepaid and Cash on Delivery easy to understand.</p>
        <h2 id="costs">Explain costs before confirmation</h2>
        <p>If a payment method changes the payable amount or delivery terms, communicate that before the final action.</p>
        <h2 id="trust">Support shopper trust</h2>
        <p>Keep payment messaging consistent with the rest of the storefront.</p>
        <h2 id="review">Review payment behavior</h2>
        <p>Understand which choices shoppers use and where confusion appears.</p>
