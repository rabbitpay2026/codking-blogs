  <Lead>Indian e-commerce shoppers may prefer different payment methods depending on trust, convenience, order value, and familiarity. A good checkout presents those choices clearly without overwhelming the customer.</Lead>

  <H2 id="choices">Present choices clearly</H2>

  Use familiar labels and make the difference between prepaid and Cash on Delivery easy to understand.

  <H2 id="costs">Explain costs before confirmation</H2>

  If a payment method changes the payable amount or delivery terms, communicate that before the final action.

  <H2 id="trust">Support shopper trust</H2>

  Keep payment messaging consistent with the rest of the storefront.

  <H2 id="review">Review payment behavior</H2>

  Understand which choices shoppers use and where confusion appears.
