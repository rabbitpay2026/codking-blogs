  <Lead>Cash on Delivery remains important for many Indian e-commerce shoppers. Verification introduces a deliberate confirmation step before fulfillment so merchants can make better-informed operational decisions.</Lead>

  <H2 id="what-verification-does">What COD verification does</H2>

  COD verification asks the shopper to confirm that the order details and delivery intent are genuine before costly fulfillment work begins.

  <H2 id="where-it-fits">Where verification fits</H2>

  The most useful point is after order creation and before packing or dispatch.

  <H2 id="experience">Designing the experience</H2>

  Use simple language, identify the order clearly, make the required action obvious, and avoid requesting unnecessary information.

  <H2 id="takeaways">Key takeaways</H2>

  * Verify before fulfillment costs are committed.
  * Keep confirmation short and understandable.
  * Use verification as one signal within the broader COD process.
