        <p className="ck-lede">Cash on Delivery remains important for many Indian e-commerce shoppers. Verification introduces a deliberate confirmation step before fulfillment so merchants can make better-informed operational decisions.</p>
        <h2 id="what-verification-does">What COD verification does</h2>
        <p>COD verification asks the shopper to confirm that the order details and delivery intent are genuine before costly fulfillment work begins.</p>
        <h2 id="where-it-fits">Where verification fits</h2>
        <p>The most useful point is after order creation and before packing or dispatch.</p>
        <h2 id="experience">Designing the experience</h2>
        <p>Use simple language, identify the order clearly, make the required action obvious, and avoid requesting unnecessary information.</p>
        <h2 id="takeaways">Key takeaways</h2>

        <ul>
          <li>Verify before fulfillment costs are committed.</li>
          <li>Keep confirmation short and understandable.</li>
          <li>Use verification as one signal within the broader COD process.</li>
        </ul>
