    <div className="ck-summary-label">Internal article template — do not publish as a blog post</div>
    <p>This page is hidden and noindexed. It exists so writers can duplicate a working article instead of building one. Duplicate it, rename it, then change only the fields listed below. Everything else is layout and must stay exactly as it is.</p>
    <p><strong>1. File name / slug.</strong> Duplicate into the blog folder. The file name becomes the URL: a file named <code>my-new-article</code> in <code>blog</code> publishes at <code>/blog/my-new-article</code>. Use lowercase words separated by hyphens. Once published, never rename it.</p>
    <p><strong>2. Title.</strong> Frontmatter <code>title</code>, and the <code>ck-title</code> heading further down. Keep both the same.</p>
    <p><strong>3. Description.</strong> Frontmatter <code>description</code>. Reuse the same sentence in the Summary box below so they stay in sync.</p>
    <p><strong>4. Category.</strong> Frontmatter <code>category</code>, and the first half of the <code>ck-eyebrow</code> line. Use one of exactly these six values: COD & RTO, Shopify, Checkout & Conversion, Payments, E-commerce Growth, or leave the article uncategorised only if none apply.</p>
    <p><strong>5. Publication date.</strong> The second half of the <code>ck-eyebrow</code> line, written as Month Year.</p>
    <p><strong>6. Read time.</strong> The <code>ck-readtime</code> line. Roughly 200 words per minute.</p>
    <p><strong>7. Hero image.</strong> The <code>ck-cover</code> block. Change the kicker and the two headline lines, and swap <code>ck-cover--template</code> for one of: <code>ck-cover--verification</code>, <code>ck-cover--rto</code>, <code>ck-cover--checkout</code>, <code>ck-cover--payments</code>.</p>
    <p><strong>8. Article body.</strong> Everything inside <code>ck-body</code>. One <code>ck-lede</code> paragraph, then H2 headings and paragraphs.</p>
    <p><strong>9. H2 headings and the TOC.</strong> Every H2 needs a unique <code>id</code>. Every entry in the right-hand On this page list must link to <code>#that-id</code>. If they do not match, the link will not scroll and the highlight will not track.</p>
    <p><strong>Then, outside this file:</strong> add a card for the article on the <code>/blog</code> homepage under the matching category heading, and add a link to it in the Blog articles list on all other articles.</p>
    <p><strong>Never edit</strong> <code>style.css</code> or <code>ck-toc.js</code>. All layout, spacing, sticky behaviour and responsive rules live there and are shared by every article.</p>
