  <section><h2>Introduction</h2><p>Cash on Delivery remains an important payment choice for many Indian online shoppers, but it also creates a different fulfillment risk from prepaid orders. A merchant may begin packing and shipping before receiving payment, so confirming that the customer genuinely intends to receive the order can be valuable.</p><h2>What COD verification means</h2><p>COD verification is a confirmation step between order placement and fulfillment. Depending on the store workflow, confirmation may happen through an OTP, a customer response, or another explicit action. The goal is not to create unnecessary friction; it is to distinguish stronger purchase intent from orders that may need attention.</p><h2>Where verification fits in the order flow</h2><p>A useful verification flow happens early enough to influence fulfillment decisions. The store receives the COD order, requests confirmation, records the outcome, and then applies its normal fulfillment rules. Merchants should keep the process clear and avoid asking customers for information that is not needed for confirmation.</p><h2>Designing a customer-friendly verification experience</h2><p>Keep instructions short, make the requested action obvious, and explain why confirmation is needed. Mobile usability matters because many shoppers complete the entire purchase journey on a phone. Merchants should also define how long an order can remain unconfirmed and how support teams should handle exceptions.</p><h2>Key takeaways</h2><ul><li>COD verification helps establish customer intent before fulfillment.</li><li>The confirmation step should be simple, timely, and mobile-friendly.</li><li>Verification should support operational decisions rather than add friction for every shopper.</li><li>Merchants should define a clear process for confirmed, unconfirmed, and exceptional orders.</li></ul><h2>Conclusion</h2><p>COD verification works best as one part of a broader order-quality process. A clear confirmation experience, sensible fulfillment rules, and consistent customer communication can help teams make better decisions without turning checkout into a complicated workflow.</p></section>
